As glyphs traverse the force-field, they undergo **phase transformations** that are **automatically projected** onto a flat or curved display surface.

* the glyph’s internal field state,

This mechanism forms a core component of a field-computational engine capable of **self-rendering behaviour**, **self-describing geometry**, and **non-numerical computation**.
